### Week 3 – Statistical Analysis and Hypothesis Testing

The **Advertising Dataset** was analyzed to investigate the relationship between advertising expenditure and Sales.

Key techniques included:

- Descriptive statistics
- Pearson correlation
- Hypothesis testing
- Simple linear regression
- Regression diagnostics
- Residual analysis

The Pearson correlation between **TV advertising expenditure and Sales was 0.7822**, indicating a strong positive relationship.

The relationship was statistically significant with a p-value of approximately **1.467 × 10⁻⁴²**.

https://github.com/vashuchandra08-tech/statistical-analysis-hypothesis-testing

### Week 4 – Machine Learning Model Development

Machine learning models were developed using the Advertising Dataset.

**Input Features:**

- TV
- Radio
- Newspaper

**Target Variable:**

- Sales

The following regression models were evaluated:

- Linear Regression
- Decision Tree Regression

The recorded test performance showed strong predictive results. The Decision Tree achieved an R² of approximately **91.45%**, compared with approximately **89.94%** for the recorded Linear Regression evaluation.

The Decision Tree feature importance was approximately:

| Feature | Importance |
|---------|------------|
| TV | 61.12% |
| Radio | 37.76% |
| Newspaper | 1.12% |

## 🔍 Key Findings

### Titanic Dataset

- The overall survival rate was approximately **38.38%**.
- Female passengers had a significantly higher survival rate than male passengers.
- First-class passengers had a higher survival rate than second- and third-class passengers.
- Passenger class and gender were important descriptive factors in survival outcomes.
- Visualization helped identify patterns that were not as clear from raw numerical data.

### Advertising Dataset

- TV advertising and Sales showed a strong positive correlation.
- Pearson correlation coefficient = **0.7822**.
- The relationship between TV advertising and Sales was statistically significant.
- Simple TV regression explained approximately **61.2%** of the variation in Sales.
- Machine learning models achieved strong predictive performance.
- TV was the most important feature in the Decision Tree model, followed by Radio.
